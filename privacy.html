Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

What we access

When you sign in a TikTok account, the app requests: basic profile info (user.info.basic), follower and like counts (user.info.stats), the list and view counts of your videos (video.list), and permission to publish videos (video.publish). For YouTube, it requests permission to upload videos and read channel and analytics statistics.

How it is used

This data is used only to post the clips you choose and to show view, follower and revenue statistics on your own local dashboard.

Where it is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the computer running the app. We do not run a server that collects this data, and we do not sell or share it with anyone.

Deleting your data

Remove the account in the app and delete its token file, or revoke access in your TikTok or Google account settings. Statistics are deleted with the app's data folder.
